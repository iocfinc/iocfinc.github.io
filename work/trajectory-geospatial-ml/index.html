Large-Scale Trajectory ML & Geospatial Systems

Engineering multi-year trajectory transition pipelines, spatial clustering across 400,000+ observations, and production ML endpoints for a regional super-app.

2024–2026 · Senior Data Scientist / Applied AI Engineer

In large-scale mobility and delivery operations, physical reality is messy. GPS signals bounce off high-rise towers, driver devices experience intermittent drops, and navigation maps often fail to capture where drivers actually park or walk when picking up orders at dense commercial centres.

At a regional super-app spanning mobility, delivery, maps, ETA, and operational decision-making, turning raw physical telemetry into reliable machine learning inputs was the central engineering challenge.

The Problem

Operating at scale across Southeast Asian metropolitan centres presents distinct spatial modeling hurdles:

  • Urban Multipath & Sensor Drift: Raw trajectory streams contain severe GPS jitter, signal loss in tunnels, and positioning drift in dense commercial hubs.
  • The “Last-100-Meter” Gap: Standard map pins point to the geometric centroid of a shopping mall or merchant block, whereas the driver’s real vehicle dropoff or parking point is situated hundreds of meters away down an alley or service ramp.
  • Offline vs. Online Parity: Features computed in batch pipelines for offline training must match streaming near-real-time production features without drift or latency degradation.

1. Multi-Year Trajectory-Transition Pipeline

Designed and maintained the foundational trajectory data pipeline that processed raw GPS feeds into clean segment transitions. The pipeline applied temporal velocity filters, dead-reckoning smoothing, and map-matching algorithms to produce high-signal inputs for downstream ETA modeling, parking-point derivation, and network segment evaluations.

2. Two-Stage Spatial Clustering for Merchant Parking Points

To bridge the last-100-meter delivery friction, built a two-stage spatial clustering engine:

  • Ingested over 400,000 trajectory observations surrounding dense merchant locations.
  • First-stage density clustering isolated stationary dwelling clusters from transit segments.
  • Second-stage geometric refinement derived approximately 9,000 precise parking and staging points across roughly 6,000 high-volume merchants.
  • Directly reduced driver search time and improved ETA accuracy for dispatch algorithms.

3. Parity Study Across 30M+ Bookings

Engineered an exhaustive parity evaluation comparing near-real-time streaming feature calculation with historical offline batch pipelines across 30M+ bookings. The audit identified edge-case divergence in timestamp windowing, establishing automated regression tests that prevented training-serving skew before deployment.

4. Production Serving & Internal Tooling

  • Co-owned the deployment, health monitoring, and cloud-budget documentation for 4+ production ML-serving endpoints.
  • Maintained a modular internal pipeline library adopted across 5 distinct production data engineering pipelines, standardizing spatial math and data validation across teams.

What This Proves

This work demonstrates hands-on ownership of large-scale ML and data systems where accuracy directly impacts real-world operational economics:

  • Turning physical noise into structured features: Designing spatial pipelines that make ambiguous real-world telemetry actionable.
  • Enforcing offline-online parity: Ensuring that models trained in notebooks behave identically when exposed to live production streams.
  • Production discipline: Pairing algorithmic innovation with cloud cost governance, documentation, and reusable internal tooling.

Current Limits

Dense indoor food courts and subterranean multi-level complexes remain difficult to resolve with satellite telemetry alone, requiring supplementary merchant-assisted location tagging and indoor beacon inputs.
