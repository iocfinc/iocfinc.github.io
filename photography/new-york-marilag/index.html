Photo story

New York, Marilag

A photo story assembled from local images already living in the workspace.

May 10, 2026

I keep coming back to cities because they are rarely neutral. They expose hierarchy, attention, and improvisation in the same frame.

New York is especially good at compressing those signals. The photograph is never only about a person or a building. It is also about timing, signage, glare, density, and the routes people improvise around formal structure.

That makes the city a useful teacher.

Observation as craft across systems, cities, and public thinking.
