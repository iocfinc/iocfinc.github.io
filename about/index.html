About

I’m Ira Fernando. I’m a Senior Data Scientist and Applied AI Engineer focused on production ML systems, real-time data products, geospatial analytics, and AI-assisted operational workflows.

I build data and AI systems that turn messy operational signals into reliable inputs for modeling, monitoring, experimentation, and product decisions. The common thread in my work is practical ownership: taking ambiguous, partially instrumented workflows and turning them into documented, monitored, experiment-ready systems that teams can operate, evaluate, and improve.

My background spans large-scale trajectory modeling, spatial clustering, and production ML endpoints at a regional super-app, alongside digital monitoring, reporting, and verification (dMRV) pipelines and reviewable AI research desks. I combine production data science experience with applied AI engineering, product thinking, and digital transformation.

This public studio is where those systems, essays, and technical build logs become public. Some emerge as longform essays. Some stay closer to notebook scale. Some are carried by photography as an observation practice. Across all formats, the goal is durable craft, explicit boundaries, and clear evidence.
