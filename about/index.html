About

I’m Ira Fernando. I work across AI systems, product discovery, and editorial thinking, and I use writing and photography as tools for sharper observation.

Most of my best work starts with noticing something carefully enough that it becomes discussable: a friction pattern in software, a weak product story, a route through a city, a habit of attention hiding in plain sight.

This site is where those observations become public. Some emerge as longform essays. Some stay closer to notebook scale. Some are better carried by photographs than prose. The point is not medium for its own sake; it is a durable practice of paying attention.
