Observation should leave evidence

May 3, 2026 · Singapore

The quickest way to weaken an insight is to detach it from the artifact, quote, screenshot, or sequence that produced it.

Notes should not only preserve conclusions. They should preserve enough of the trail that you can re-evaluate the conclusion later.
